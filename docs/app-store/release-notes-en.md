**Fyndplats – carefully picked deals at smart prices**

Fyndplats is the Swedish online store for anyone who wants real deals without the
hunt. We hand-pick the range — quality goods at prices that actually live up to
the word "deal". Now you get all of Fyndplats in your pocket: browse, search,
shop and track your order right in the app.

**What you'll find**
• Clothing & fashion for women, men and kids
• Home & interior
• Kitchen & housewares
• Beauty & health
• Accessories, bags and much more

The range is updated continuously with new finds — open the app often so you
don't miss a thing.

**Shop with confidence**
• **Klarna** – pay now, later or split your payment
• **Free shipping** on all orders over SEK 499
• **30-day right of return** – changed your mind? Sending it back is easy
• **Swedish customer service** that helps you all the way
• Secure payment and reliable delivery, every time

**App features**
• **Browse** smoothly across all categories and curated deals
• **Search** quickly for exactly what you're after
• **Cart** that stays with you between visits
• **Klarna checkout** built right in – smooth and secure
• **Push notifications** for new deals, campaigns and back-in-stock items
• **Order tracking** – follow your delivery from order to doorstep

**Why Fyndplats?**
We believe great deals shouldn't take hours of searching. So we do the work for
you: pick the products, set smart prices and make sure the whole purchase — from
first tap to delivered parcel — feels safe. Swedish e-commerce, Swedish customer
service and deals you actually want.

Download Fyndplats today and start finding.
